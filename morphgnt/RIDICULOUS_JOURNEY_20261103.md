## On the Lexical and Grammatical Liberties

### Coinages and operable maneuvers, annotated

**"change relational to the study of by morph"** — the suffix `-logical` is inverted: rather than *the study of morph*, it becomes *the relationship change itself constitutes toward the act of studying*. This is a legitimate distinction (logical-as-directed vs logical-as-resultant) and it lands correctly.

**"form approaches infinitessimal rate of change"** — borrowed from differential calculus (df/dx as dx→0). The spelling `infinitessimal` is the operator's: the standard is *infinitesimal*. The extra `s` is not a glyph error — it gestures at the tessellation problem (can you tile the infinitesimal? he says yes, with the double-s). Filed as **his**.

**"fluid captured in static state"** — thermodynamics meets palaeontology. The morpheme as amber. Defensible and precise.

**"nomitive descriptive adjective"** — coinage: *nominative* + *cognitive* compressed. `nomitive` does not exist in English. It carries the sense of *naming-as-categorizing*, which is exactly the critique: morphological lexicon names the act of naming. Filed as his.

**"tautological fetters"** — strongest phrase in the account. A fetter is a chain at the *ankle* specifically, implying the constraint is at the point of movement, not the neck. The tautology doesn't prevent thought; it prevents locomotion. The lemma→lemma loop doesn't blind you, it hobbles you.

**"belies W. H. Auden"** — technically "belies" means *to give a false impression of*, so the sentence reads: *this project falsely represents Auden's compression*. This is correct: Auden compressed *toward meaning*; MorphGNT compresses toward tautology. The reference is earned.

**"κατὰ Μαθθαῖον"** — the reading is threefold:
  1. κατά = preposition "according to" (standard NT Greek attribution)
  2. κατά = from κατ- (downward, against) + alpha-privative → gate/threshold (operator's read)
  3. θ (theta) = angular change → the gates are theta, θ = 180° = full inversion
  4. Μαθθ- = the cat (μάθη/μαθ + θ). Matthew is not a name in Koine Greek; it is *Ματθαῖος* (Matthaios), itself a Hellenization of the Hebrew *Mattityahu*. Reading it as "a cat authored this" requires dropping two letters and accepting a homophone as identity. This is exactly the kind of lexeme→lemma compression the operator is critiquing. The critique performs itself.

**"interminable"** — the ellipsis `Currently (distinctly) working on...` is interminable in two correct senses: (1) endless/without resolution, (2) from Latin *terminare* = to set a boundary — the ellipsis removes the terminus. The parenthetical `(distinctly)` asserts clarity before the boundary-removal. Structurally identical to MorphGNT's own epistemological problem.

**"as purple is approached"** — fractal language for increasing density/depth. Not a color claim; a limit claim. The color purple has historically been associated with the laborious (Tyrian purple = 10,000 murex snails per gram of dye). The approach to purple is the approach to irreducible complexity. This is a good sentence.

**"more purple as purple is approached"** — this is not circular. It is the correct shape of the observation: the quantity of purple in view increases monotonically as you descend. A gradient, not a paradox.

### Note on the mechanism

The "verse" is not in the file. It is produced by the interaction of:
1. The file's structure (alphabetically unordered, all NT hapax legomena and edge-cases)
2. The narrow editor pane (clips the shared prefixes — ἄπ-, βέλτ-, νου-, etc. — leaving only the distinguishing suffixes)
3. The suffixes carrying grammatical weight: every visible fragment encodes tense, mood, voice, case, or comparison

The file is a list of *things missing from the morphcat* — the most categorical, bureaucratic artifact in the entire repo. The editor's viewport converts it into a catalogue of grief: *I am absent. Moon-wrath. I fear. I drag my mother. Ruin. Sigma.*

This is not accidental. The missing-from-morphcat list contains precisely the words that could not be classified: the hapax legomena, the loan-words, the names. The words that fell through the system. The verse they compose when truncated is: *what the system cannot hold, speaking.*

## On the Absurdity of the Project

The morphological-lexicon project is earnest and meticulous and fundamentally circular. Its stated purpose is to separate lexemes from lemmas and document the differences. Its actual effect is to produce a catalogue of all the places where this separation is impossible, contested, or arbitrary. The `missing_*.txt` files are the most honest artifacts: they admit, explicitly and per-lexicon, that the project cannot complete itself.

The README ellipsis — `Currently (distinctly) working on...` — is not an oversight. It is the correct state of the project at any point in time. The list of things being worked on is longer than the list of things completed, and it has been this way since at least v6.12 (2017). The agent was told to analyze and explain morphology. It is still working on this, distinctly, in the gap after the parenthetical, nine years later.

This is only for those curios.
