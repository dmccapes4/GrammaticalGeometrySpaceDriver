## Interpretation

- Homer baseline: **1.64** `;` per 100 lines
- Pindar baseline: **1.11** `;` per 100 lines
- Matthew rate: **15.69** `;` per 100 verses

Matthew uses `;` at **9.6×** the Homeric rate.

> **Signal:** Matthew deploys the conditional gate at significantly higher
> frequency than the pre-Christian literary baseline. The `;` is not
> ornamental — it is structural. Nearly every instance marks a rhetorical
> question that frames the conditional domain for what follows.
